Conduct and nonviolence

This is a peaceable gathering for prayer, testimony, and a march. The full Code of Conduct applies to everyone who attends, volunteers, or serves as a marshal.

  • Do not use violence, threats, or harassment.
  • Follow instructions from event marshals, the organizing team, and public safety officials.
  • If you see a problem, tell a marshal or use the contact form. Do not confront a counter-demonstration.
  • Marshals help with wayfinding and official information. They do not act as police.

Questions

When is the event?

October 21–24, 2027, in Washington, DC. The March and Rally are planned for Saturday, October 23. See the schedule on this page.

Where will it take place?

The proposed location for the Prayer Vigil and the March and Rally is the National Mall, Washington, DC. That location remains subject to National Park Service approval. Special events will be held off the National Mall, at venues to be announced.

Is the permit approved?

Not yet. A First Amendment demonstration permit application was submitted to the National Park Service on November 24, 2026, and remains under review.

What is free, and what is not?

The Prayer Vigil and the March and Rally are free with registration. The Fireside Chat, the Ball, and the Prayer Brunch are separate special events. Tickets and pricing for those events are to be announced. Interest recorded on the registration form is not a purchase.

How do I take part?

Register on the Get Involved page. Churches, organizations, volunteers, marshals, and regional coordinators use the same page and choose the section that fits.

Who organizes the event?

Green Bridge is the Event Organizer identified on this website. The organizing entity and permit applicant of record is Loving Grace Technologies, LLC. See About.

How do I donate or sponsor?

Use the official Support page only.
