Special Events

Alongside the march itself, the four days hold three special events. You can request tickets for these events through the form below.

Fireside Chat, Ball and Prayer Brunch are paid, ticketed events. Tickets will be sold separately from the free march ticket.

Fireside Chat — Thursday, October 21, 2027 — Time and venue to be announced.

The Ball — Saturday evening, October 23, 2027 — Time and venue to be announced.

Prayer Brunch — Sunday, October 24, 2027 — Time and venue to be announced.

Select every special event you plan to attend. Your selections are recorded and listed in your confirmation.

Special event ticket requests do not guarantee admission, reserved space or seating, and requesting tickets here does not complete a purchase. Ticket prices and purchase details, along with times, venues and access arrangements, will be announced on the Updates page as they are confirmed.
