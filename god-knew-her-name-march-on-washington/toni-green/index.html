Toni Green

International Queen of Blues

Toni Green is a Memphis-born soul and R&B vocalist. Her gospel blues testimony "God Knew Her Name" is the song this march carries to Washington.

The Song

Before there was a march, there was a song. "God Knew Her Name" is sung by Toni Green, the International Queen of Blues, a Memphis soul and blues vocalist, and was released in May 2026.

It is a gospel blues testimony for the people who prayed, served and carried pain quietly, and were still seen by God: the mothers, grandmothers, church mothers, prayer warriors, caregivers, widows and quiet servants who kept showing up even when the world did not always see them.

The song's title says "Her," but its message was never only for women. It is for everyone who has ever felt unseen and kept going anyway: people overlooked, misnamed and forgotten by the world, who were never forgotten by God. God knew their name before the world ever mislabeled them.

As the song was being developed into a stage play, Toni envisioned herself as both a phoenix and a tree, with roots, a trunk and limbs, seeing in others what they could not always see in themselves. That vision became the Phoenix Tree concept. The original artwork, depicting a golden tree with phoenixes rising behind it, now serves as the march's emblem.

Bring the Song to Your Choir

The free Choir Resource Kit helps choirs, churches, praise teams, women's ministries, school choirs and community programs bring "God Knew Her Name" to their own services and events.

The kit includes: Lyric Sheet; Choir Arrangement Notes; Chord Chart; Practice Materials Guide; Praise Dance & Liturgical Movement Notes; God Knew Her Name Sunday Service Outline; Toni Green Artist One-Sheet.

Through her official artist website, Toni Green invites churches, school choirs, praise teams, women's ministry programs and community choirs to rehearse and perform the song for worship services, women's events, healing services, school programs, choir programs and community events.

Please use the requested artist credit: "God Knew Her Name" by Toni Green.
