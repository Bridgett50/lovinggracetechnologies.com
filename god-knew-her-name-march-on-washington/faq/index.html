Frequently Asked Questions

When is the event?

Friday, October 22, 2027: Prayer Vigil, 5:00 PM–9:00 PM Eastern Time. Saturday, October 23, 2027: March and Rally, 10:00 AM–3:00 PM Eastern Time.

Where will the event take place?

Proposed location: National Mall, Washington, DC. Event schedule and location are from the organizer's records and remain subject to applicable approvals and coordination.

Is the permit approved?

A First Amendment demonstration permit application was submitted to the National Park Service on November 24, 2026. The application is currently under review. Final locations, routes and operational arrangements remain subject to applicable approvals and coordination.

How do I participate?

Begin by registering your interest through this website. Individuals register on the Attend page; churches register on the Churches page; organizations register on the Organizations page.

How do I volunteer?

Apply on the Volunteer page. Tell us about your experience, availability and the areas in which you would like to contribute.

How do I serve as a march marshal?

Apply on the Marshals page. Assignments will be made through the event's organizing team following the appropriate review, training and operational planning.

How do I donate?

Donations will be received through Square. The official Square donation link will be added to the Donate page when it is created. See the Donate page for how to contribute now.

How can my business or organization sponsor the event?

Sponsorship applications are open. Apply on the Sponsors page. Confirmed sponsors will be recognized in the official sponsor directory.

Who organizes the event?

Organizing Entity: Loving Grace Technologies, LLC. Event Organizer: Bridget Sander. Event Coordinator: Toni Green. Leadership information is from the organizer's records.
