Businesses and organizations may apply to sponsor the gathering. Privacy

This form is sent by email to the organizing team. We use what you send only for this gathering. We do not sell it, and this site does not use third-party advertising trackers. Because submissions arrive by email, we may not be able to change or delete a message once it has been received. If you need a correction, use the contact form and we will do what we can.

Store

As part of a special event or demonstration permit, sales on the National Mall are limited to books, newspapers, leaflets, pamphlets, buttons, and bumper stickers containing a message related to the activity. Apparel and recordings, including T-shirts, hats, and CDs, are sold online only and are never sold on the Mall.

Song merchandise and recording sales are available. Buy recordings and Toni Green’s artist merch through the official links below. March-branded items that are not yet listed will be added here when they are ready. This site does not list a price that is not already published by the official store or purchase link.
