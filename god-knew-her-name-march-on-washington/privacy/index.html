Privacy

This website is the official site of the God Knew Her Name March on Washington.

Forms on this site are sent by email to the organizing team. There is no separate participant database on this website. We use what you send only for this gathering. We do not sell it, and this site does not use third-party advertising trackers.

Because submissions arrive by email, we may not be able to change or delete a message once it has been received. If you need a correction, use the contact form and we will do what we can.

Donations are processed by Square through the account of Loving Grace Technologies, LLC. Song purchases made through Cash App or Toni Green’s store are handled by those services, not by this website’s forms.
