For Artists

Artists, Come Forward

This march takes its name and message from the song "God Knew Her Name," and we invite artists to express their interest in participating.

If you are an artist who would like to participate in the God Knew Her Name March on Washington, October 21–24, 2027, we invite you to come forward and tell us who you are.

The gathering is built around faith, music and testimony. We welcome expressions of interest from:

  • Singers and soloists
  • Choirs and praise teams
  • Musicians and bands
  • Praise dancers and liturgical movement artists
  • Spoken word artists

How participation works

Participation begins with the interest form below. Submitting the form is an expression of interest. It is not a booking, and it does not guarantee a performance or a place in the program.

The organizing team reviews every submission as programming is confirmed. The full schedule is still being built, and many activities will take place at the same time across the four days. Artists who are selected for a part in the program will be contacted with details as those details are confirmed. Details of the program will be announced on this site as they are set.

If your artistry speaks to the experiences of people who have prayed, served and carried pain quietly, and who were never forgotten by God, we would like to hear from you.

Tell us who you are and what you do. Every submission is read by the organizing team.

Privacy notice

This form collects only the information the organizing team needs for the purpose shown. Participant data is never sold. This website uses no third-party advertising trackers. Submitted information is accessible only to authorized organizing personnel. You may request correction or deletion of your information, subject to applicable retention requirements.
