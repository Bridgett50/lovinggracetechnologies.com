Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone at the gathering, including attendees, volunteers, marshals, invited guests, and the organizing team.

A peaceable gathering

This is a faith-centered prayer vigil and march. We expect everyone to help keep it peaceable.

  • Do not bring weapons.
  • Do not use violence, threats, or harassment.
  • Do not interfere with emergency lanes, accessible routes, or the work of public safety officials.
  • Treat other people, including those you disagree with, without abuse.

Following official direction

Follow instructions from event marshals, the organizing team, and public safety officials, including the National Park Service and police. The gathering remains subject to the permit and to applicable law.

If there is a problem

Tell a marshal or contact the organizing team. Do not confront a counter-demonstration. Marshals help with wayfinding, official information, and a peaceable event. They do not act as police.

Program and speech

The organizers set the program. The gathering is nonpartisan and does not endorse candidates or parties. Campaign activity and commercial activity must follow the rules that apply on the National Mall and at any off-Mall venue.

Violations may result in being asked to leave, and may be referred to public safety officials when required.

If you plan to attend, register and read this Code of Conduct before you travel.
