Secure Your Free Ticket

The march is free. Register to receive your confirmation, which serves as your free ticket, and stay informed as gathering details are confirmed.

One registration form records your participation plans for the four-day gathering, October 21–24, 2027, in Washington, DC.

Register below for your free march ticket, request tickets for the special events you plan to attend, and tell us if you are traveling with a group or bus.

Your Free Ticket

The March and Rally are planned for Saturday, October 23, 2027. The proposed location is the National Mall, Washington, DC.

Admission to the march is free, and your ticket is free.

When you register, you receive a confirmation. That confirmation is your free ticket. Keep it, printed or on your phone.

Your registration also records the days and events you select below, so the organizing team can plan for participation.

What your ticket does not promise: Registration does not guarantee admission, reserved space, seating or a place in any particular activity. Registration confirms attendance interest and helps the organizing team plan.

Event locations, access arrangements and programming remain subject to applicable approvals and coordination, and the full schedule is pending.

Special Event Tickets

Alongside the march itself, the four days hold three special events. You can request tickets for these events through the same registration form below.

Select every special event you plan to attend. Your selections are recorded with your registration and listed in your confirmation.

Fireside Chat — Thursday, October 21, 2027 — Time and venue to be announced.

The Ball — Saturday evening, October 23, 2027 — Time and venue to be announced.

Prayer Brunch — Sunday, October 24, 2027 — Time and venue to be announced.

Special event ticket requests do not guarantee admission, reserved space or seating. Times, venues and access arrangements will be announced on the Updates page as they are confirmed.

Coming by Bus

Groups planning to travel to Washington by bus can register their transportation arrangements through the march website.

If your group or organization is bringing a bus, register the bus on the Transportation page. The registry records your departure city and state, vehicle information and estimated passenger count.

If you are riding on a bus someone else has organized, register here for your own free ticket and name your group or bus captain in the field provided on the form.

Every rider registers individually. Your group's transportation registration and your individual registration help the organizing team plan for participation.
