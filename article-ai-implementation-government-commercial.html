AI implementation for government and commercial teams

What government and commercial teams should require from AI implementation: verified work, kept evidence, and approval before money or property moves.

Verified work before it moves

Nothing the AI drafts should move money or property without a check against what was actually agreed. That is the principle behind Sovereign Mesh, our verified-handoffs work, a TRL 2 concept and an Encountering Innovation 2027 applicant: custody and authorization verified before the handoff, not assumed after it. Sovereign Mesh is a concept and an applicant, described here as what it is, not as a shipped product.

Evidence kept with the work

A decision should keep its reasons: what was claimed, where it came from, and what was checked. We work from primary sources, keep the record with the work, and do not present a test result as a client outcome.

Approval with a person

AI drafts; a person approves. The person who approves sees what was actually agreed, not only what the system drafted, and no agent grants itself approval for a consequential move.

Built around your workflow and records

Our work covers custom AI agents, AI assistant setup, workflow and AI automation, documentation and research, and AI implementation training built around the team's own tools, workflows, and approvals.
