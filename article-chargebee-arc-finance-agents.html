Research and Analysis

Finance agents that show their work before anything is committed

Chargebee launched Arc on Nov 18, 2026. Three finance agents handle contract ingestion, receivables, and revenue recognition, and every action is shown, cited, and held for review before anything is committed.

On Nov 18, 2026, Chargebee announced Arc, a new agentic platform that runs complete finance workflows as one connected, traceable thread, from contract to invoice to revenue. At launch, Arc's pre-built agents solve three finance jobs: contract ingestion, receivables, and revenue recognition. A workspace called Agent Studio lets teams build custom agents on the same foundations. Arc is open for early access now.

Three agents, one thread

The contract ingestion agent reads a signed contract and brings a proposed billing setup and open questions to the finance team, with every extracted field linked back to its source, including the customer, items, prices, entitlements, price ramps, billing dates, and payment terms. The receivables agent builds a context graph of each customer's contracts, usage, and payment history, then prepares collections work, such as drafting the reply to a promise to pay or a question about a usage charge. Human teams approve, edit, or reject each proposal, and the agent works within each company's own rules and escalation thresholds. The revenue recognition agent breaks each contract into performance obligations, applies company policy, including prepaid credits, overages, and rollovers, and reallocates price when a contract changes, so teams can trace how each amendment changed revenue.

The governance layer is the product

Arc runs on four building blocks: tasks, drafts, comments, and citations. Tasks break complex work into steps, coordinate it across tools, teams, and time, and keep a clear record of each step and who took it. Drafts give teams a safe place to check proposed work, so nothing irreversible happens until someone signs off. Comments let agents flag ambiguity and collaborate with users in context. Citations show where each value came from, so review and verification go faster. Every action is shown, cited, and held for review before anything is committed.

Arc carries each piece of finance work from start to finish as a single thread, logging every step with its author and its rationale, so the audit trail is complete whether a human or an agent did the work. When a human corrects an agent's treatment, a built-in learning loop remembers that correction the next time. Arc also works beyond Chargebee, pulling context from and acting across systems such as contract management, CRM, and email.

Why finance first

Finance teams rely on different systems of record for auditable, defensible numbers, but those systems do not capture the day-to-day operational context scattered across Slack threads, customer contracts, collections emails, and employees' heads. Chargebee says hundreds of its customers have already built agents, dashboards, and automations with its MCP release, and Arc grew from what those teams built. The quote from co-founder and CEO Krish Subramanian in the release puts it plainly: finance teams need systems that provide a shared foundation for humans and agents, and a standard of consistency and accuracy for every unit of work.

What to watch

Whether early-access customers report that the citation trail holds up under real audit, and whether the learning loop's memory of corrections stays visible to the team that made them. The release states no price and no general availability date. Early access is open now, and Chargebee points inquiries to witharc.ai.
