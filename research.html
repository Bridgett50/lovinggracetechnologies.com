Research and Development

Loving Grace Technologies is an independent deep-technology research and development company. We pursue federal research funding, competitive innovation programs, and intellectual property development, alongside selected client work.

Lead research program

Sovereign Mesh: Verified Handoffs

Research objective: a system for independently verifiable authorization, custody transfers, evidence integrity, and operational continuity when connectivity is unreliable.

Status, stated plainly: Technology Readiness Level 2. The concept is formulated. The integrated system is unproven. There is no validated integrated prototype today.

The first workflow is contents pack-out and restoration handoffs, where property changes hands between organizations and the record of who authorized what must hold up later.

The test plan is 20 controlled handoffs, including unauthorized requests, offline operation, and later reconciliation, with results recorded rather than assumed. The target is a controlled physical handoff prototype in April 2027.

Second Seat, Professional Packout, and ClaimVision provide reusable components and use cases. They do not yet constitute a validated integrated prototype.

Sovereign Mesh is an Encountering Innovation 2027 applicant, submitted November 2, 2026. Encountering Innovation is a route to defense technology scouts and research partners. It does not itself award funding. Sovereign Mesh has no weapons function.

Working systems

Research with working demonstrations

Second Seat

Approval research with a working demo. AI drafts each payment, then a separate checker compares it against the owner's own words and blocks anything that does not match. In a PayPal sandbox demonstration, a fake bank-change request was blocked and a clean $480 payment was approved and captured. Enforcement happens at the action gateway, not by screening agent words.

Method

How research is done here

Our research method is: Brief. Verify. Map. Model. Resource. Ask. Command.

Claims are graded before they are used

Verified, distorted, or unsupported. A claim that cannot be checked is labeled as unchecked, not presented as fact.

Checking is separate from drafting

Second Seat demonstrates our research approach: separating AI drafting from independent checking and human approval. We apply this principle when designing other multi-agent systems. Tamper-evident is not the same as truthful, and we build to that distinction.

Confidentiality is a design rule

An AI's ability to access or understand information never establishes permission to disclose it. Every disclosure needs authorization of its own.

We state the maturity of each program plainly. Research takes time. Concepts are presented as concepts, and working systems are presented as working systems.
