Who you are buying from

Discovery Scout is sold by Loving Grace Technologies, LLC, Grenada, Mississippi, United States. Payments are processed by Square for Loving Grace Technologies, LLC. Contact: bridgettsanders50@lovinggracetechnologies.com.

What Discovery Scout is

Discovery Scout is online planning software for legal outdoor hunts, such as gems, gold panning, fossils, shark teeth, meteorites, and metal detecting for modern items. Concierge planning is planning help from a person. Area scores describe geology and documented public sites. They are not a promise or a chance of finding anything.

What Discovery Scout is not

  • It is not a travel agency. We do not book, sell, or resell tickets, tours, permits, lodging, transportation, or gear.
  • Dig sites, guides, parks, hotels, and stores are independent. You book and pay them directly, on their terms and refund rules. Loving Grace Technologies does not charge anything on their behalf and is not responsible for their prices, closures, or services.
  • It does not give permission to enter land, dig, or collect. You are responsible for following site rules, landowner wishes, and collecting laws.
  • It is not for archaeological or artifact hunting, or for taking cultural artifacts.

Plans and prices

  • Done-for-you plan: $19 per hunt plan, paid once.
  • Concierge planning: $49 per trip, paid once.
  • Scout: $9 a month or $79 a year, renews automatically until you cancel.
  • Pro: $19 a month or $149 a year, renews automatically until you cancel.

Prices are in U.S. dollars. Any tax shown at checkout is added by Square.

How a plan is delivered

After you pay, we email you at the address you gave at checkout to collect your trip details (what you want to look for, where you are starting, your dates, and your party size). We deliver your plan by email within 3 business days of receiving those details.

Subscriptions and cancelling

Scout and Pro renew at the end of each month or year at the price you signed up for. You can cancel at any time with the manage-subscription link in your Square receipt email, or by emailing us. Cancelling stops the next renewal. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Refund policy

  • Before we start: if you ask before we begin your Done-for-you or Concierge plan, we refund the full amount.
  • After delivery: if your plan has a factual mistake we made (for example, a wrong site, a closed site we listed as open, or a missing legal checklist), tell us within 14 days of delivery. We will correct it, or refund you if we cannot.
  • Subscriptions: if you ask within 7 days of your first payment and have not received a done-for-you plan, we refund that first payment. Renewals are not refunded for partial periods, but cancelling stops the next charge.
  • Not refundable by us: weather, site closures, finding nothing, or anything you paid to a dig site, guide, hotel, or store. Ask that business about its own refund rules.
  • Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Square. Email bridgettsanders50@lovinggracetechnologies.com with your receipt.

Your safety

Outdoor hunts can involve heat, water, weather, and rough ground. Plans are a starting point. Use your own judgment, follow posted rules, and change or cancel your trip if conditions are unsafe.
