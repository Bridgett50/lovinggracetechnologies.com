Second Seat

It turns what a small-business owner agrees to in a meeting into PayPal payments. AI drafts each payment, then a separate checker compares it against the owner’s own words and blocks anything that doesn’t match, like a changed bank account or an inflated amount. Nothing is paid until the owner approves it with one tap, and every payment keeps the quote it came from as its receipt.
