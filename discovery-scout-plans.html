Good to know

  • Discovery Scout is a planning tool. Area scores describe geology and documented sites. They are not a chance of finding anything.
  • We deliver Done-for-you and Concierge plans by email within 3 business days after we have your trip details.
  • We do not sell tickets, tours, permits, lodging, or gear. Anything you pay a dig site, guide, or hotel, you pay them directly, under their own terms. Loving Grace Technologies charges nothing on their behalf.
  • A plan does not give permission to enter land, dig, or collect. Check the site rules and collecting laws before you go.
  • Not for archaeological or artifact hunting. Metal detecting is for modern items only.
