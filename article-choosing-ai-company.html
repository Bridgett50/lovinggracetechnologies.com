Choosing an AI implementation company

Ask these six questions before you hire anyone to build with AI. The answers tell you who keeps you in charge.

1. Who approves what the AI does?

A good answer: a person does, before anything is sent, paid, or changed, with approval gates built into the work. Be careful with any answer that treats approval as optional or as something to add later.

2. Is it built around my workflow and records?

A good answer: yes, custom to your rules and your records. Be careful with one-size templates sold as custom work. If the builder cannot describe your workflow back to you, the build will not fit it.

3. What happens when the AI is wrong?

A good answer: an independently checked workflow, retained evidence, visible errors, and documented testing of what the controls can and cannot catch.

4. Do I keep control of my files and infrastructure?

A good answer: ask whether self-hosted options are available, where your files will reside, who controls the infrastructure, and what happens to your data and system if the engagement ends.

5. Is training built for my team?

A good answer: sessions built around your tools, workflows, and approvals, not recorded generic material. Training should fit the team that receives it.

6. Can you show the work?

A good answer: demos, documentation, and a clear scope before work starts. Verify, then decide. Claims without something to look at are not evidence.

That order is how we work: brief, verify, map, model, resource, ask, command. We scope from your actual facts, build around your workflow, and keep approval with you.
