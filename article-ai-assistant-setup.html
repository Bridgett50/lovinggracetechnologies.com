AI assistant setup, explained

Your AI assistant should know your work before you start typing. Setup is what makes that true.

An AI assistant out of the box knows nothing about your business. It can draft, answer, and research, but it does so generically, and you spend your time re-explaining context it should already have. Setup closes that gap. It connects the assistant to your work, your files, and your preferences, sets its instructions and boundaries, and leaves you with a working assistant tuned to you.

What setup covers

  • Instructions for how the assistant should behave, in your patterns and your tone
  • Boundaries for what it must never do alone, including anything sent, paid, or changed
  • Your files, records, and context, so it works from your facts instead of guessing
  • The assistant you already use, including Muse, Grok, ChatGPT, and Claude
  • A working session showing you how to use it well, fitted to your real work

What changes after setup

You stop re-explaining. The assistant drafts in your patterns, checks against your records, and asks before anything moves. The hours of trial and error go away because the tuning is done for you, fitted to your work, current on the day it is done.

Setup is not a course

We do not sell recorded material. Setup is done for you, on your assistant, with your work. What you get is a configured assistant and the knowledge to use it, not a video to watch.

If your assistant still feels generic, setup is the missing step.
