About Loving Grace

Where Consciousness Meets Technology.

Loving Grace Technologies, LLC is a technology company based in Grenada, Mississippi. We work at the intersection of consciousness and technology: sacred by design, scientific in method, and humane in every decision. We build technologies that protect consciousness and expand human potential.

The company was founded in 2026 by Bridgett Sanders, who leads product and engineering. Our work spans personal AI assistants, meeting automation, and creative tools for musicians, guided by rigorous inquiry and compassionate design.
