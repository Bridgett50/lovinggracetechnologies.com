About Loving Grace

Where Consciousness Meets Technology.

Loving Grace Technologies is a Mississippi company building human-centered deep tech. We combine scientific rigor with human wisdom: tools that help people work, document, and decide with more clarity, and with compassion in the design.

We are an R&D company. We also take on selected client work — systems planning, websites, custom software, and AI implementation training — always with people in charge of the decisions that matter.

Our Name

Machines of loving grace

Loving Grace Technologies takes its name from Richard Brautigan’s 1967 poem “All Watched Over by Machines of Loving Grace,” and from Dario Amodei’s later essay of the same title. Both imagine technology that watches over people with care, not control — a world where people, nature, and machines live in harmony, and humans keep final authority.

That is the standard we build to: tools that extend what a person can do, without taking away what only a person should decide.
