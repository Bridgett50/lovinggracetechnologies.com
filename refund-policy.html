Discovery Scout Refund Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026

This policy explains refunds and cancellations for Discovery Scout, sold by Loving Grace Technologies, LLC (Grenada, Mississippi, United States). It is part of the Discovery Scout Terms of Use: https://lovinggracetechnologies.com/terms.html

Questions or refund requests: bridgettsanders50@lovinggracetechnologies.com

The short version

  • Free plan and the waitlist: free. Nothing to refund.
  • Done-for-you plan ($19) and Concierge ($49): full refund if you ask before we start your plan. After delivery, if the plan has a real error we cannot fix, you get a full refund.
  • Scout ($9/month or $79/year) and Pro ($19/month or $149/year): cancel any time. Monthly plans: a full refund of your first monthly charge if you ask within 7 days. Yearly plans: a full refund if you ask within 14 days of the yearly charge.
  • We do not charge you for tickets, tours, digs, lodging, or travel from other businesses, including operators on Viator or GetYourGuide. Refunds for those come from the business you paid, under its own rules.
  • Discovery Scout does not guarantee finds. Not finding anything is not, by itself, a reason for a refund.

1. One-time plans: Done-for-you ($19) and Concierge ($49)

Before we start. If you ask for a refund before we begin preparing your plan, we refund the full amount.

Late delivery. If we do not deliver your plan by the time shown at checkout, you may cancel and receive a full refund, or keep the order and wait.

Errors in the plan. Ask within 14 days of purchase. If your plan has a real error (for example, the wrong location, a site listed as open that was already closed when we prepared the plan, or a missing part of what the plan includes), tell us what is wrong. We will correct it within 3 business days. If we cannot fix it, or you would rather not wait, we refund the full amount.

Concierge. If you cancel a Concierge trip after we have started the planning work but before we deliver the finished plan, we refund the full amount minus $19 for the Done-for-you plan, and we still send you that plan. After the Concierge work is delivered, the error rules above apply.

Trip changes. If your trip is canceled or moved because of weather, a site closure, or a change in your own plans, our fee is not refunded after the plan is delivered, because the planning work is done. Email us and we will update the plan for a new date within 60 days of purchase at no charge, once.

2. Subscriptions: Scout and Pro

Cancel any time. Email bridgettsanders50@lovinggracetechnologies.com with the subject "Cancel Discovery Scout," or use the cancel option in your account or receipt if one is offered. Canceling stops the next renewal. You keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

Monthly plans ($9/month Scout, $19/month Pro). If you ask within 7 days of your first monthly charge, we refund that charge in full and end the subscription. After that, monthly charges are not refunded, but you can cancel at any time to stop the next one.

Yearly plans ($79/year Scout, $149/year Pro). If you ask within 14 days of a yearly charge (the first one or a renewal), we refund that charge in full and end the subscription. After 14 days, the yearly charge is not refunded, and your access continues until the end of that year.

Billing mistakes. If we charged you twice, charged the wrong amount, or charged you after you canceled, we refund the mistaken charge in full, whenever you tell us.

3. Things we do not charge for, so we cannot refund

Loving Grace does not take payment for any third-party ticket, tour, dig, guide, park admission, lodging, transportation, gear, or travel. That includes operators listed on Viator, GetYourGuide, Expedia, or other platforms, and fee-dig sites and parks you book yourself. If you need a refund for any of those, contact the business you paid. Its own refund and cancellation rules apply. We are happy to help you find the right contact.

4. How to ask for a refund

Email bridgettsanders50@lovinggracetechnologies.com with:

  • the subject "Discovery Scout refund";
  • the email address you used to buy;
  • the plan and the date of purchase; and
  • a short note on what went wrong (for error refunds).

We reply within 3 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method. Your bank or card issuer usually shows the refund within 5 to 10 business days after we send it.

5. Please contact us before a chargeback

If something is wrong, please email us first. We can usually fix it or refund you faster than a card dispute.

6. Your legal rights

This policy does not take away any refund or cancellation rights you have under the law where you live. If the law gives you more, you get the more generous rule.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The version in effect on the date of your purchase applies to that purchase.
