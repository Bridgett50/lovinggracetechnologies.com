The AI hardware company changed leaders. The roadmap did not move.

The release gives no reason, and the earnings date did not move.

On Nov 12, 2026, NVIDIA announced that Jensen Huang had resigned as President, Chief Executive Officer, and a member of the Board, effective immediately. The announcement came from the company's Board of Directors, published as a press release through its investor site. It is short, and it is worth reading exactly as written, because what it does not say matters as much as what it does.

Colette Kress, the company's Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer, agreed to serve as Interim Chief Executive Officer. She keeps the chief financial officer role while she holds the interim job, until a permanent successor is named. Kress has been NVIDIA's chief financial officer since joining the company in September 2013. Before that, she spent nearly 25 years in finance roles at technology companies, including Cisco and Microsoft. The Board said it is initiating a search for a permanent chief executive and will consider internal and external candidates. No timetable for the search was given.

The Board also appointed Harvey C. Jones as its first-ever Chair of the Board. Jones has been an entrepreneur, technology executive, and active venture investor for over 30 years, including serving as Chief Executive Officer and Executive Chairman of Synopsys.

The release gives no reason for the resignation. It thanks Huang for his contributions over 33 years and calls NVIDIA one of the most consequential technology companies in the world. It does not connect the departure to any investigation, dispute, or business result. Any account that supplies a reason is going beyond the company's own statement, and this article does not repeat one.

What the release does say, twice, is that the work continues. Jones, speaking as Chair, said the Board has full confidence in Kress and the leadership team, and that the company expects its operations, customer commitments, and product roadmap to continue without interruption. Kress said the focus remains on serving customers, partners, developers, and shareholders, and on advancing the work that has made NVIDIA a leader in accelerated computing and AI.

The calendar did not move either. NVIDIA will report its third-quarter fiscal 2027 financial results on Tuesday, November 17, at approximately 1:20 p.m. PT, as previously scheduled, followed by its earnings call at 2 p.m. PT. That puts Kress in front of investors five days after taking the interim role, with the company's numbers, not its leadership story, as the subject.

For teams that build on NVIDIA hardware, the practical read is narrow. The chips, the software stack, and the supply commitments are run by the same leadership team the release names, and the release says the roadmap continues without interruption. The open questions are the ones the release leaves open: who the permanent chief executive will be, whether the search looks inside or outside, and what the Nov 17 results say about demand for the hardware the whole AI field runs on. A founder-CEO who led a company for 33 years leaving with immediate effect and no stated reason is a real event. It is also, on the company's own statement, an event with no announced change to what the company ships or when.

What to watch

The Nov 17 earnings release and call, the first public forum for the interim chief executive, and any word on the permanent search, including whether the Board names a candidate from inside the company or outside it.
