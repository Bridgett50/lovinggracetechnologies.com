Verified handoffs: custody and authorization before the move

A verified handoff checks custody and authorization before money or property moves, and keeps the evidence with the work.

A handoff is the moment something changes hands: money, property, records, or responsibility. Most systems record the handoff after it happens. A verified handoff checks it before it happens, and keeps the evidence with the work.

That is the idea behind Sovereign Mesh, our verified-handoffs work and an Encountering Innovation 2027 applicant. Sovereign Mesh is currently a TRL 2 concept. No integrated prototype or production deployment is verified. It is a concept and an applicant, described here as what it is, not as a shipped product. The first intended workflow for Sovereign Mesh is contents pack-out and restoration handoffs, covering the movement, storage and return of household contents after a loss.

A verified handoff answers three questions before the move:

Custody

Who has the item now, and who is receiving it? The design calls for verifiable custody records at each handoff, identifying the parties involved and preserving evidence of the transfer. This is a design objective, not a claim that an integrated operational system is already deployed.

Authorization

Who approved this move, and on what evidence? Drafting and approving are separate jobs. The person who approves sees what was agreed, not only what the system drafted.

Evidence

What record travels with the work? The intended record captures what moved, when the transfer occurred, who authorized it and what evidence supported the decision. Keeping evidence with the work is intended to make subsequent reviews, reconciliation and dispute investigations more traceable.

The same pattern applies beyond packout work. Any time money or property moves on the strength of a record, the check should come first: verify the custody, verify the authorization, then move, and keep the record.

Tell us about a handoff in your work that needs checking before it moves. We will show what verification would change.
