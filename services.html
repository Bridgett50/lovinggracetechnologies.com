Our Services

An R&D company that helps teams plan, build, document, and implement technology — with people still in charge.

Automation

Workflow and AI automation

AI-assisted workflow design, intake, prompt systems, and approval workflows for repetitive tasks. We help structure document-heavy operations into clear processes and practical automation plans, with human review built into decisions.

Planning

Computer systems planning

Requirements, tool selection, integration maps, and implementation documentation. We plan for the systems a team already has and the ones it needs next.

Web design and development

Websites and web applications

Design and build of websites and web apps for businesses, artists, and public-facing work, as well as custom product software. Selected examples appear on our Work page as screenshots.

Custom training

AI implementation training

We train teams to implement AI using systems we build, not off-the-shelf courses. Curriculum and sessions are custom-built around the tools, workflows, and approvals that team will actually use.

Writing and research

Documentation, evidence, and research strategy

Operating procedures, runbooks, evidence matrices, decision records, review checkpoints, and source-backed research for government and commercial teams, including concept papers and proposal support.

Software

Packout software

Software for contents inventory, packout, storage, packback, condition documentation, and claims reporting. Loving Grace Technologies builds and supports the software.

Consulting

Small Business Consulting

We help small businesses get ready for an AI-powered world. We look at how your business runs today, find where AI can save you time and money, and build a clear, practical plan to put it to work. We also help with the foundations around it: planning, registrations, and contracting readiness, so you’re set up to grow.
