2. What Discovery Scout is

Discovery Scout is a planning tool and planning service for legal outdoor hunts in the United States, such as gem and mineral digging, gold panning, fossil hunting, shark-tooth hunting, meteorite hunting, metal detecting for modern items, and storm or expedition planning.

Depending on the plan you choose, Discovery Scout can give you:

  • spot reports built from open public data (for example geology, land status, historic maps, and documented public sites), with scores and the reasons behind each score;
  • a legal checklist for the kind of hunt and the kind of land;
  • a list of nearby sites where the operator allows visitors to keep what they find, with the prices and rules we found on those sites' own pages;
  • a written trip plan with an itinerary, gear list, estimated costs, weather forecast, and links to the operator's own booking page and to lodging searches; and
  • a filled-in landowner permission letter for private land that you can send yourself.

5. Concierge planning help

Concierge is planning help, not booking. For a Concierge trip, we:

  • prepare your Done-for-you plan;
  • check the site's current hours, prices, and rules, using the site's own public pages and, where needed, the site's public contact channel, before you go;
  • explain how and where to book directly with the operator, and answer your planning questions about the trip; and
  • help you prepare and follow up on a landowner permission letter for private land, which you send in your own name.

We do not make reservations, sign agreements, or pay anyone for you, and we never hold your money for a third party. We do not share your contact details with a site or landowner unless you ask us to. Operators set and change their own prices, hours, and rules; what we report is what we found at the time we checked.
