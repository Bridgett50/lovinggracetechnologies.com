Privacy Policy

Last updated November 10, 2026

What We Collect

This website has no sign-up forms, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking cookies. We do not sell personal information.

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include, and we use it only to reply to you.

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, to deliver and secure the site. Fonts are served by Google Fonts. The Work page embeds a YouTube video in privacy-enhanced mode; YouTube’s own privacy policy applies when you play it.
