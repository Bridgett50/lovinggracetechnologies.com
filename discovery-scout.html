Discovery Scout

A planning tool for legal outdoor hunts. See plans and prices or join the waitlist.

Planning tool

Plan the hunt. Read the reasons.

Discovery Scout helps people plan legal outdoor hunts using commercially allowed open data: geology, land status, and documented public sites. It scores areas and explains the reasons for each score. It is a planning tool, not a guarantee of finds.

Planning topics are gems, gold, fossils, shark teeth, meteorites, metal detecting of modern items only, and storm and expedition planning.

  • Scores come with reasons you can read
  • The data is commercially allowed open data
  • Metal detecting is limited to modern items
  • It is not for archaeological or artifact hunting, and it is not a way to take cultural artifacts
  • It does not give permission to enter land, dig, or collect. Site rules and collecting laws are yours to check before you go
